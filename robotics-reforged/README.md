# Robotics Reforged

**A rework of Factorio's robot tiers, with new tiers where the progression needs them.**

Vanilla gives you one logistic robot and one construction robot, and from then on every upgrade
comes from research that lifts all of them at once. Robotics Reforged turns robots into a
progression you build through: better robots you unlock, craft and swap in.

> **In development.** Nothing has been released yet, and what the tiers are is still being
> worked out. This page will say what the mod actually does once it does it.

## Requirements

Factorio. Both 2.1 and 2.0 are supported. Space Age works but is not required.

# Pure Modules

Every tier-3 module makes you pay twice. Speed modules burn energy *and* cost you quality.
Productivity modules drink power and smoke up the sky *and* slow the machine to a crawl.
You take the deal because there's nothing better.

Pure modules are what comes after. They're stronger, and they only charge you once: power,
and a little pollution. No speed penalty. No quality penalty.

## The modules

A new top tier of modules, above tier 3. Nothing existing is touched — Pure modules sit
above the vanilla progression rather than replacing it.

- **Speed** — faster than tier 3, without the quality hit.
- **Productivity** — more output than tier 3, without the speed hit.
- **Quality** — better quality than tier 3, with no drawback at all.

The bonus grows with a module's own quality; the cost doesn't — a legendary Pure module
runs no more expensive than a normal one, for noticeably more effect.

There's no Pure efficiency module — tier 3 already has no drawback left to remove. The
Pure quality module needs the Quality expansion; the other two work without it.

## The beacon

One beacon, built for the tier: bigger than the ordinary beacon, with more module slots
and a much wider reach. It takes speed and efficiency modules of any grade, vanilla or
Pure.

What it won't do is stack freely — the benefit of adding more of them on one machine
flattens out well before you'd run out of room to place them, so past a certain point
another one just costs power for nothing extra. That's the trade: reach and clean modules,
instead of a wall of beacons. Ordinary beacons still stack independently, so ringing a
machine with both kinds gets you both — at the floor space and power that costs.

Productivity and quality are switched off by default, because no beacon transmits either
in the base game and beaconed productivity especially is a big swing. Two settings turn
them on; until then, the beacon simply won't accept those modules rather than take them in
and do nothing.

## Building them

The beacon comes first — its technology unlocks the three module technologies. Each
module is built from a stack of its matching tier-3 modules, so upgrading is a clear step
up from what you're probably already producing. The beacon itself is built from ordinary
beacons plus processing components — with Space Age, it instead asks for materials off
every planet, the same deal Vulcanus gives the foundry.

With Space Age, everything here is built on Aquilo by default and sits at the end of that
expansion's tech tree; without it, the tier follows space science instead. Once built,
modules and the beacon both work anywhere — a setting lifts the Aquilo restriction.

## Compatibility

Base game. Space Age isn't required, but is supported if installed. Quality is optional
too — without it, the Pure speed and productivity modules are unaffected and the Pure
quality module simply doesn't exist.

Nothing vanilla is renamed or removed, so it's safe to add to a running save. Two things
it does change: ordinary beacons stop accepting Pure modules (a setting turns this back
off), and every module in the game is given the artwork a beacon needs to show it in a
slot, since most ship without one.

Removing the mod mid-save deletes any Pure modules and beacons already placed, as with
any mod that adds items.

## Roadmap

Nothing here is promised, but it's what's being considered next:

- **More modules and beacons** — maybe a single "god" module that folds every bonus
  together, or another beacon variant.
- **Compatibility with other mods** — a good reason to add further tiers of modules or
  beacons tied to other planets, if the right mod comes along.
- **More languages** — the mod is English only for now.

Version: 1.0.8
Date: 2026-08-15
  Graphics:
    - The Pure beacon is redrawn with deeper shading and crisper edges, so its
      coils, panels and instruments read at normal zoom instead of blurring
      together.
    - On Aquilo the frozen beacon now shows snow lying on the machine, rather
      than a pale film washing the whole building out.
    - A module's colour no longer paints a flat patch over its socket. It tints
      the housing and lets the metal underneath show through.
    - The beacon's wreck is darker, rustier and more burnt, matching the other
      ruins in the game.
    - The module and beacon icons are sharper, and the dark fringe around their
      edges is gone.
    - The Pure beacon's containment well is now open and lit. Its glow was
      sealed inside the casing before and never actually visible.
    - New machinery on the containment ring: a condenser stack on one side, a
      control head with a sight glass on the other, and a coolant line arching
      between them.
    - The Pure beacon's colours are warmer and stronger, so it sits alongside
      the game's own machines instead of reading flat grey.
    - The Pure beacon's core sits at a deeper blue, and after dark it shines
      and casts light on the ground around it instead of going flat with the
      rest of the machine.
  Changes:
    - Releasing for Factorio 2.0. Version 1.0.9 is the Factorio 2.1 build of
      the same mod.

Version: 1.0.0
Date: 2026-08-06
  Major Features:
    - Initial release.
    - Added the Pure speed module: +70% speed with no loss of quality, for +50% energy and
      +25% pollution.
    - Added the Pure productivity module: +15% productivity with no slowdown, for +50% energy
      and +25% pollution.
    - Added the Pure beacon: a wide-area beacon built for the tier, with a supply area of
      21x21 tiles against the ordinary beacon's 9x9. Takes speed and efficiency modules of
      any grade, vanilla or Pure.
  Features:
    - Added a technology for each Pure module, costing 3000. Without Space Age they follow
      space science at the end of the tech tree.
    - Each Pure module is built from six tier 3 modules, an efficiency module 3, processing
      units and low density structures.
    - Added a technology for the Pure beacon, the prerequisite for all three Pure module
      technologies. It's built from four ordinary beacons, processing units and refined
      concrete.
    - Stacking Pure beacons on one machine caps at three: a fourth transmits nothing extra,
      trading a wall of beacons for one wide ring instead.
    - Added two startup settings: restrict Pure modules to the Pure beacon, on by default,
      and let the beacon transmit productivity, off by default.
  Graphics:
    - Added full animated graphics for the Pure beacon: counter-rotating containment rings,
      a bobbing crystal core, and electric arcs while it's transmitting.
  Compatibility:
    - [quality] Added the Pure quality module: +4% quality, with no drawback at all. A
      legendary one reaches +10%.
    - [quality] Added a startup setting to let the Pure beacon transmit quality, off by
      default.
    - [space-age] Pure module research follows the quantum processor, and their recipes call
      for quantum processors.
    - [space-age] Everything in this mod builds on Aquilo by default, the same deal Vulcanus
      gives the foundry; once built, modules and the beacon work anywhere. A startup setting
      lifts the restriction.
    - [space-age] The Pure beacon's recipe asks for one item from every planet: tungsten,
      carbon fibre, a supercapacitor and a quantum processor.
    - [space-age] The Pure beacon frosts over on Aquilo like other machinery there.

Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-09-12
  Major Features:
    - Initial release, for Factorio 2.1.
    - Adds the quality recycler: a 4x4 recycler that scraps items two and a half times as fast
      as the normal one. Each result has a 12% chance of higher quality, no modules needed. It
      has five module slots and draws more power than a normal recycler.
    - [space-age] Unlocked once you have Vulcanus, Fulgora and Gleba science. Built from a
      recycler plus parts from all three planets.
    - Without Space Age, unlocked after the rocket with space science, and built from a recycler,
      low-density structures and electric engines.

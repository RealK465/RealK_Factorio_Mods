Version: 0.1.0
Date: ????
  Major Features:
    - Added two quality tiers above legendary: mythic and celestial.
  Features:
    - Mythic quality is unlocked on Aquilo, celestial quality after promethium science.
  Changes:
    - Epic quality now comes from Fulgora instead of Gleba.
    - Legendary quality now comes from the first three planets instead of Aquilo.
  Balancing:
    - The early quality tiers come quicker than in vanilla, so the longer ladder still
      fits inside a normal game. Reaching legendary takes about a third less material.
      From epic upward the odds are vanilla's.
    - An assembling machine 3 runs at 4 on mythic and 5 on celestial.
    - Every quality tier is now about 25% better than the one below it, all the way up.
      Epic is slightly stronger than in vanilla and uncommon slightly weaker; rare and
      legendary are unchanged.
  Graphics:
    - Redrew every quality icon and every quality technology icon, vanilla's included, so
      all seven tiers match.
  Gui:
    - The quality picker keeps its row of buttons instead of collapsing into a dropdown
      once there are more than five tiers.

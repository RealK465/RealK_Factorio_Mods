# .ai-support index — Extra Qualities

Read this first. One line per file: what is in it, and when to read it.

- **`decisions.md`** — what is settled and why: the mod's name and identity, the dependency
  set, where each tier is gated, the two new prototypes' field set, and the quality-picker
  fix. Read before changing anything already decided.
- **`balance.md`** — the ladder's numbers and where they came from: the levels, the retuned
  odds, the measured cost of every tier, and the design rule the numbers serve. Read before
  touching `next_probability`, a `level`, or a technology cost.
- **`art-direction.md`** — the quality glyphs and the technology dies: the pip language, the
  measured vanilla geometry and palette, and the generators that produce them. Read before any
  icon work.
- **`deferred.md`** — open questions and parked work. Read before the next session's first
  change; the balance is unplayed and that is the largest item on it.
- **`journal.md`** — dated sessions, newest first. Read to catch up on what happened and why.
- **`analysis/index.md`** — verified engine findings, with the version each was checked at.

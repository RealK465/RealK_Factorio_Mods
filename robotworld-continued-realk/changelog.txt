---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-10-16
  Major Features:
    - Robot World Continued by thinkong, ported to Factorio 2.1.
  Features:
    - Added a setting to start wearing power armor MK2 with a reactor, personal roboports, batteries, an exoskeleton and night vision.
    - With Early robots on, destroyed buildings leave ghosts for robots to rebuild from the start.
  Changes:
    - Fewer settings: robots are tuned per type, early robots are one choice, roboport charging includes energy storage, and the fixed roboport radius settings are gone.
    - Settings and early research from Robot World Continued do not carry over.
    - Cannot be enabled together with Robot World or Robot World Continued.
  Compatibility:
    - [Krastorio2] The quick start kit adds 2 boilers and 4 steam engines, and 100 wood for basic tech cards when they are in use.
    - [Krastorio2] The starting power armor's reactor comes loaded with uranium fuel cells.
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed that the quick start kit only reached the host, and only when the intro was skipped. Every new player now gets it.
    - Fixed that a fractional robot cargo size or stack size stopped the game from loading.
    - Fixed that a higher robot cargo size or speed stopped the game from loading when another mod caps them.
    - Fixed that hidden robots from other mods were changed too.
    - Fixed that robot stack size skipped robots from other mods whose item has a different name.
    - Fixed that a robot from another mod with no health value set stopped the game from loading.
    - Fixed that a robot health multiplier of 0 stopped the game from loading. The lowest value is now 0.01.
    - Fixed that cheap early robots, chests and roboports recycled into the ingredients of their normal recipes.
    - Fixed that roboports with less energy storage could stay off for good after a blackout.
    - [bobelectronics] Fixed that cheap early recipes used Bob's old circuit name and stopped the game from loading.

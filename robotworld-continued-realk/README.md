# Robot World Continued (RealK)

**Tune your robots and roboports with startup settings. Optionally get robots early with cheap recipes and a quick start kit.**

A fork and Factorio 2.1 port of thinkong's Robot World Continued. Every setting is in Settings > Mod settings > Startup. All multipliers default to 1 (no change).

## Settings

**Early robots** picks what you can build from the start: construction robots, then logistic robots too, then the full logistic system. Roboports, passive provider chests and storage chests come with the first step, and destroyed buildings leave ghosts for your robots to rebuild. With "Cheap early recipes" on, they cost only iron plates, gears and electronic circuits. Four matching early technologies are added: basic personal logistics, basic logistic trash slots, basic worker robot speed and basic worker robot cargo size. The vanilla worker robot speed 1 and cargo size 1 research follow after these.

**Quick start kit** gives every player in a new freeplay game inserters, poles, drills, furnaces, assemblers, steam power, labs, coal, provider and requester chests, 200 logistic robots, 50 construction robots and 10 roboports. Works in multiplayer. With Krastorio 2 it adds 2 more boilers and 4 more steam engines, plus 100 wood for basic tech cards when your game uses them.

**Start with power armor** puts every new player in a power armor MK2 with a portable fission reactor, 2 personal roboports, 4 batteries, an exoskeleton and night vision. With Krastorio 2, where the reactor burns fuel, it comes loaded with uranium fuel cells.

**Robot settings** set speed, cargo size, battery and energy use separately for construction and logistic robots, plus health and stack size for both. Cargo size is rounded to whole items.

**Roboport settings** cover the logistic area, the construction area, charging (energy storage grows with it) and idle power. "Only change the vanilla roboport" leaves roboports from other mods alone.

**Inserter speed** is a single multiplier for all inserters.

## Compatibility

Works with or without Space Age. Requires Factorio 2.1.

With Bob's Electronics, the cheap recipes use its basic circuit board instead of electronic circuits.

Not compatible with "Robot World" or "Robot World Continued", which change the same things, so only one can be active. Settings and research from those mods do not carry over.

Hidden robots added by other mods are left alone.

## What changed from the 2.0 original

- Runs on Factorio 2.1.
- New setting: start in power armor MK2 with robot-building equipment.
- With Early robots on, destroyed buildings leave ghosts from the start.
- Fewer settings: one set per robot type, and Early robots is now a single choice.
- With Krastorio 2, the quick start kit adds more steam power and wood for tech cards.
- Quick start now works for every player in multiplayer. Before, only the host got it, and only when skipping the intro.
- Fractional cargo multipliers no longer stop the game from loading.
- Cheap early robots, chests and roboports no longer recycle into expensive parts.
- Hidden robots from other mods are no longer changed.

For Factorio 2.0, use the original [Robot World Continued](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/robotworld-continued).

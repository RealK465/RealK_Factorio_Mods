Version: 0.4.0
Date: ????
  Features:
    - Production chains: click an ingredient beside the machine to make it too, in a cell of its
      own. Its ingredients can be added the same way.
    - The whole chain comes as one blueprint, the cells side by side on one power network.
      Robots carry the ingredients between the cells.
    - Each cell has its own machine, modules and recipe. Everything else is shared by the chain.
    - A cell's icon turns red when it makes less than the chain uses.
    - Choose how many cells to build of each item. The balanced count shows in green, and the
      Balance button sets them all at once.
  Gui:
    - The ingredient amounts window lists every cell of the chain.
  Info:
    - A chain makes up to 8 items, in up to 32 cells.

Version: 0.3.0
Date: 2026-11-28
  Gui:
    - The planner window is much smaller. Every option is still one click away, with its name

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.2.0
Date: 2026-11-28
  Features:
    - When one inserter can't keep up with the machine, the cell gets more, each with its own
      chest. They go along the bottom first, then up the side, where the beacons step out.
    - Inserter speed and load come from your inserter, its quality and your research, so modded
      inserters work too.
  Changes:
    - The crafting rate now shows what the cell really makes, with a warning when the inserters
      still hold it back.
    - Several ingredient chests share a large request, and each gets a small one whole.
    - Several output chests share the slot limit.

Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-11-25
  Major Features:
    - Initial release.
    - Pick an item and get a mall cell as a blueprint: the crafting machine with its modules,
      inserters, a requester chest for ingredients, and output chests.
    - Set ingredient requests by a number of crafts, by minutes of crafting, or by hand for
      each ingredient.
    - When the cell can make better quality, a second output chest catches the better items
      while the first chest holds items at the recipe's quality.
    - Limit stock by chest slots, by a count across your logistic network, or not at all.
    - Add optional beacons beside and above the machine, and an optional electric pole.
    - Recipes with a fluid ingredient get a pipe along the back of the cell. Cells placed
      side by side share one pipe line, fed from either end.
    - Every picker has its own quality and remembers your last choice.
  Info:
    - The shortcut button appears once requester chests are researched.
    - Recipes that make a fluid, or that need two fluids, are not supported yet.
    - Fuel-burning machines like the biochamber are not supported yet.
    - Red messages block the Place button. Orange messages are warnings you can build through.

Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-09-13
  Major Features:
    - Initial release, for Factorio 2.1.
    - Adds the quality assembler: a 3x3 assembling machine that crafts 60% faster than an
      assembling machine 3. Each result has a 12.5% chance of higher quality, no modules needed. It
      has five module slots and draws more power than an assembling machine 3.
    - Replaces an assembling machine 3 in place with an upgrade planner, keeping the recipe, the
      modules and the inserters.
    - [space-age] Unlocked after Aquilo, with cryogenic science. Built from an assembling
      machine 3, two quality module 3s, lithium plates and superconductors.
    - Without Space Age, unlocked after the rocket with space science, and built from an
      assembling machine 3, two quality module 3s, low-density structures and electric engines.

## Why can't I see my machines?

The planner only offers what you already researched: items, machines, recyclers, belts, inserters, chests, modules, poles and qualities you cannot build yet are hidden, and every picker defaults to the best researched option. A modded machine appears as soon as the technology that unlocks it is researched.

To see everything anyway, tick **Show unresearched items** in the planner's own settings — the **Settings** button in its title bar, beside the close button. It is a per-player setting, so it changes the lists only for you, and it is also in Settings → Mod settings → Per player if you would rather set it there.

## Where did the recycler and chest pickers go?

A picker only appears when there is something to choose between. A vanilla game has one recycler, one requester chest, one passive provider chest and one pipe, so those four are not shown and the planner simply uses them. Each comes back on its own as soon as a mod adds an alternative.

If you want them anyway — to build them at a quality of your own, say — tick **Show all build options** in the planner's settings. Every picker is shown then, with its quality box beside it.

## Why is my fluid recipe refused?

Recipes with one fluid ingredient work: each machine gets a pipe run ending in underground stubs beneath the loop's top and bottom belts, and you connect your fluid supply to either side with an underground pipe of your own. Two kinds still cannot be looped: recipes needing two different fluids (only ammonia rocket fuel in the base game — plain rocket fuel loops fine), and recipes that return a fluid beside the item, like the quantum processor, because the loop would also have to drain that fluid away.

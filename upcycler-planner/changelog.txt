Version: 0.1.0
Date: ????
  Major Features:
    - Initial release.
    - Pick an item and target quality, configure the loop, and place the whole thing as ghosts.
    - One machine per quality tier with recyclers feeding ingredients back in below the target.
    - Quality modules fill machines and recyclers below the target; the top machine gets productivity when allowed.
    - Build options: belt, quality module, electric pole, and whether chests trash unrequested items.
    - Machines, recyclers, modules and poles can each have their own build quality.
    - Supports modded recyclers with different sizes and eject directions.
    - Electric poles planned for full power coverage and wired into one network. Higher-quality poles need fewer placements.
    - Selectors default to the best researched option and remember your last pick.
  Info:
    - The shortcut appears once recycling is researched.
    - Fluid recipes are not supported yet.
    - Red messages disable Place; orange messages are warnings you can build through.

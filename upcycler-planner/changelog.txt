Version: 0.6.0
Date: 2026-08-26
  Features:
    - Circuit limits: you're now able to configure a minimum reserve of lower quality items to keep in the loop.
    - Circuit limits: you can also configure the maximum of items to produce (so the loop stops when limit is reached).
    - Lower quality items now stay in buffer chests by default instead of requester chests
      (so they can be used by construction robots (uncheck "Use buffer chests" to keep the old behavior)).
  Gui:
    - The Place button now works while the Settings or Limits window is open.
    - The chest and module pickers are named for their jobs now: Ingredient, Stock, Relay,
      Output and Overflow chest, and Final machine module.
  Locale:
    - Reworded the planner's labels, tooltips and messages to be shorter and clearer.
  Changes:
    - Releasing for Factorio 2.0. Version 0.6.1 is the Factorio 2.1 build of the same change.

Version: 0.4.0
Date: 2026-08-18
  Major Features:
    - The planner now gives you a blueprint instead of a placement tool. You can see the whole
      loop before you place it, move it around, and rotate or flip it.
  Changes:
    - The three chest pickers are now hidden. The planner uses the biggest chest you have
      researched. Tick Show all build options in Settings to choose them, or to set their quality.
    - Pressing E in the planner window now does the same as clicking Place.
    - Undo takes the whole loop back in one step.
    - The blueprint stays in your hand after placing, so you can stamp the same loop again.
    - The blueprint disappears when you clear the cursor.
      Drag it into your inventory instead if you want to keep the design.
    - Releasing for Factorio 2.0. Version 0.4.1 is the Factorio 2.1 build of the same change.

Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-08-16
  Major Features:
    - Initial release.
    - Pick an item and target quality, configure the loop, and place the whole thing as ghosts.
    - Quality modules fill machines and recyclers below the target; the top machine gets productivity when allowed.
    - Build options: belt, quality module, electric pole, and whether chests trash unrequested items.
    - Machines, recyclers, modules and poles can each have their own build quality.
    - Supports modded recyclers with different sizes and eject directions.
    - Electric poles planned for full power coverage and wired into one network. Higher-quality poles need fewer placements.
    - Selectors default to the best researched option and remember your last pick.
  Info:
    - The shortcut appears once recycling is researched.
    - Fluid recipes are not supported yet.
    - Red messages disable Place; orange messages are warnings you can build through.
  Changes:
    - Releasing for Factorio 2.0. Version 0.1.1 is the Factorio 2.1 build of the same mod.

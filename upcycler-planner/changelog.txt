Version: 1.1.0
Date: 2026-09-07
  Features:
    - The circuit limits now live on a constant combinator inside the loop, so you can change
      the minimums and the maximum in place without placing a new blueprint. To lift the
      maximum in place, type a big number: a C row at 0 stops the loop.
    - Switching the combinator off pauses a loop that has a maximum. A new "Start paused" option
      in the Limits window places it switched off, handy while bots are still bringing the
      modules. Without a maximum the switch only removes the reserves, so leave it on.
    - Two lamps and a display panel show what the loop is doing: blue while it runs, green when
      the output chest is full or the loop is paused, and the panel's icon is visible on the map.
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed that a reserve could drop below its minimum by up to one inserter hand. The
      minimum on the combinator now includes one hand size per column of that quality, and
      the inserters that draw from the reserve are held to that hand. A new "Hand size" field
      in the Limits window lets you change it.

Version: 1.0.0
Date: 2026-08-29
  Features:
    - If the chosen item supports productivity modules, the planner now mixes them
      with the best ratio to boost output (see: https://wiki.factorio.com/Tutorial:Quality_upcycling_math).
    - A new sub config frame allows you to configure manually the ratio of productivity modules.
    - You can now configure the quantity of columns for each quality tier to boost output.
  Optimizations:
    - Planning loops with extra modded qualities and high column counts is now much faster
      (generating a loop with 8000 columns was reduced from 750s to 5s).
    - Changing modules, ratios or circuit limits no longer re-solves the pole layout.
  Gui:
    - The planner now shows stats of how many items and how much time are required to produce
      1 item of the target quality (works better on 2.1 with dedicated native API functions).
    - The stats show the machine and recycler counts, hover over them to see the footprint.
    - Warnings and errors under the planner now get their own lines.
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed that the first click on a picker could trigger a layout re-solve.
    - Fixed that editing circuit limits did not update the planner's warnings.
    - Fixed that planning could freeze the game re-solving poles forever on a beaconed plan.
    - Fixed that a recycling recipe listing the same ingredient in two rows was refused.
  Changes:
    - Releasing for Factorio 2.1.

Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-08-16
  Major Features:
    - Initial release.
    - Pick an item and target quality, configure the loop, and place the whole thing as ghosts.
    - Quality modules fill machines and recyclers below the target; the top machine gets productivity when allowed.
    - Build options: belt, quality module, electric pole, and whether chests trash unrequested items.
    - Machines, recyclers, modules and poles can each have their own build quality.
    - Supports modded recyclers with different sizes and eject directions.
    - Electric poles planned for full power coverage and wired into one network. Higher-quality poles need fewer placements.
    - Selectors default to the best researched option and remember your last pick.
  Info:
    - The shortcut appears once recycling is researched.
    - Fluid recipes are not supported yet.
    - Red messages disable Place; orange messages are warnings you can build through.
  Changes:
    - Releasing for Factorio 2.0. Version 0.1.1 is the Factorio 2.1 build of the same mod.
